# One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch

Entry for the [One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The pitch

> A toaster that sues you for emotional damages every time you burn the bread, serving the legal papers through its crumb tray.

One sentence, 125 characters. It names the tiny everyday annoyance
(burnt bread), the ridiculous invention (a toaster that sues its owner)
and its silly mechanism (legal papers served through the crumb tray).

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) validates the pitch against
the gate — UTF-8 text, 1–220 characters, exactly one sentence — and
writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## Candidates considered

Ten pitches were written and the freshest was picked; runners-up:

- A Wi-Fi router that faints dramatically whenever the microwave runs, then revives itself with a gasp and a full apology.
- A printer that prints a tiny resignation letter instead of your document every time it runs out of ink.
- A fridge light that goes on strike at 3 a.m., leaving you to raid the leftovers in total darkness as punishment.
- A pen that writes "I told you so" in the margin every time you cross a word out.
